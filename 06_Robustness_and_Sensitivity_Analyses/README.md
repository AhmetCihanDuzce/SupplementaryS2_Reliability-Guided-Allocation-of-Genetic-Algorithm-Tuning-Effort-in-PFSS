# Robustness and Sensitivity Analyses

This directory contains the machine-readable analyses supporting the manuscript's robustness, interpretation, and limitation statements. These analyses do not alter the original prespecified 24-target confirmatory protocol.

Contents include:
- exact leave-one-instance-out historical reliability sensitivity across all nine anchors and 90 single-anchor deletion scenarios;
- prospective action stability and reliability-to-direct-center-loss association under those deletions;
- interpolation and action-threshold sensitivity;
- GREEN finite-grid random-configuration diagnostics;
- intervention-only (AMBER+RED) direct-center versus policy continuous-loss, tolerance, recovery, and reverse-discordance results for the ORIGINAL conservative prospective implementation;
- normalized computational-work proxies for that original conservative prospective implementation;
- inference hierarchy and empirical-reference re-evaluation diagnostics;
- a summary record linking the final recommended staged policy to its 3,767-run operational budget and matched-budget validation in Section 05.

`Robustness_and_Sensitivity_Summary.json` collects the principal manuscript-level values. Detailed CSV files preserve target-level or scenario-level results.
